Register this RP

A one-time step (Phase 1). The RP proves its identity D to the IdP and receives an anonymous credential, then registers (cid, DC) at the broker. After this you can sign in. Re-registering is safe — it issues a fresh credential.

The two exchanges

Step 1 · RP → IdP

The RP blinds its identity key and asks the IdP to sign it. It gets back the domain credential DC and the secret credential SC — an anonymous credential the IdP itself cannot later recognise.

Step 2 · RP → Broker

The RP hands (cid, DC) to the broker, which stores it under an internal handle and can from then on route logins for this RP — while only ever seeing a randomized form of the credential.
