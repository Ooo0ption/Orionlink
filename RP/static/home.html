Authorization management

Revoking this app’s authorization is a user ⇄ IdP action — this RP cannot revoke on your behalf, and the broker is not involved. The button below hands you over to the IdP, where you confirm the RP’s identity D inside the trusted TCA and then revoke the session pseudonym acid (paper Table 1, Authorization revoke).

🚫 Manage / revoke at the IdP opens the IdP’s authorized-clients page
Revoking deletes the IdP-side authorization record for this acid. Because acid is a per-session pseudonym, the IdP still cannot tell which RP it belongs to — only you can, after decrypting D in the TCA.
